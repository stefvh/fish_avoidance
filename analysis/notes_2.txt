GENERAL PROBLEMS WITH THE EXPERIMENT DESIGN
-------------------------------------------

- The "treatment" is inconsistent. Here our treatment is the behavior of the robot.
- No "control group". No data when the fish is alone in the tank, without a robot.

Response variables (Y_continuous):
    - Fish speed (continuous in [0, ...])
    - Fish cosinus of retreat vector "cos(vec_ret)" (continuous in [-1, 1])

Notes on Binarization of Response variables (Y_binary):
    - Fish speed:
        If fish_speed < THRESHOLD => Fish is stationary
        Otherwise => Fish is moving
    - Fish cos of retreat vector:
        If cos(vec_ret) > 0 => Moving away from robot
        Otherwise => Moving towards robot

Predictor variables (X):
    - Robot-fish distance
    - Robot speed
    - Interaction : robot_speed / distance
    - Robot cosinus of approach vector "cos(vec_app)"
    - Fish distance to the closest wall
    - Fish orientation relative to the robot
        E.g. is the fish facing the robot directly?

Considerations/Problems:
    * Time-delay
        - Y_{t+delta} ~ B*X_{t}

        - How to decide on the THRESHOLD for fish_speed
    * Predictor variables

        How well can we rely on the orientation to be correctly tracked?

            E.g. if cos(vec_ret) = -1 -> The fish is facing the robot directly
