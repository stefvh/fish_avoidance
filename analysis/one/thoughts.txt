To explain the models that we have
acceleration_fish: distance significant, but not speed_robot
    -> This means that the fish will accelerate higher just by the robot being close,
    and being directed at the fish
speed_fish: distance not significant, but speed_robot is
    -> So, although it has higher accelerations, the distance is not,
    This means the fish deploys higher speeds regardless of where the robot is,
    as long as the robot is moving towards the fish and with greater speed, greater response
    It could mean that the fish was mostly stationary when the robot approached, leading to acceleration for distance
    But then the fish maintains the speed for a longer time, possibly accelerating later again to escape,
    while the distance between robot and fish increased.
    What it basically means: that the "escape" velocity (at close distance) isn't significantly different than the "avoidance" velocity (at higher distance)
    where both strategies try to move away from the robot, and as the robot goes faster and towards the fish, the fish has to go faster
